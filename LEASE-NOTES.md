# Lease notes — the source lease, as it bears on the model

The clauses of the source lease that the model applies as lease terms, held on every lessor in
`data/fleet.json` (generated by `data/generate.ts`) and applied in `calc/`. The lease view on
screen (`ui/components/LeaseView.tsx`) quotes them, with each tail's return conditions. Only what is recorded
here; the rest of the source lease is not reproduced. Where the wording is quoted it is marked so;
otherwise it is a summary of the clause.

## 12.2 — Replacement engines and parts (the replacement test)

**12.2(a)(i)(2)**, quoted: an Engine installed as a permanent replacement must be *"in the same or
better operating condition, has no less hours or Engine LLP life available until the next scheduled
checks, inspections, overhauls and shop visits and has the same or greater value and utility as the
replaced Engine"*.

**12.2(a)(ii)**, summary: replacement Parts are held to the same test.

**As applied** (`replacementBlock` in `calc/levers.ts`): a swap passes only if the incoming unit has
no less life than the outgoing one on every clock the lease names for that component — hours and
cycles to its next shop visit (engines), months, cycles or APU hours to its next overhaul (gear,
APU), and LLP cycles. Both units are read as the records stand today, on the receiving tail's
clauses. Otherwise the swap is refused, in the levers and in the what-if, with the clause and the
shortfall: *"not a permitted replacement under Clause 12.2(a): ESN-6519 has 12,355 fewer LLP cycles
than ENG1"*. On a swap between two tails the test applies on both tails, each against its own
lessor's clause — so it passes only when the two units have the same life on every clock.

"Same or greater value and utility" is not tested separately: the model has no unit valuation
beyond its life, so life on every clock is the test it can apply.

Every current lessor carries this strict form (`replacementTest: 'strict'`).

## 12.3(b) — Notice of a planned engine removal

Summary: any planned engine removal needs 90 days' notice to the lessor.

**As applied** (`noticeBlock`, `forcedNotice`, `swapOption`): an engine swap has to happen by the
earlier of the outgoing engine running out and the shop-slot deadline; its decide-by date is 90
days before that, and the deadline's working cites the clause.

The clause asks notice of a **planned** removal. A planned swap whose notice can no longer be given
is refused: *"not possible under Clause 12.3(b): a planned engine removal needs 90 days' notice"*.
A removal **forced** by the engine running out before handback is not planned, and the clause
cannot block it: if the full notice no longer fits, notice goes to the lessor now, short, and the
working says so — *"a removal forced by ENG2 running out, not a planned one, so Clause 12.3(b)'s 90
days cannot be given in full: notice goes to the lessor now, 35 days ahead, 55 days short — a
conversation with the lessor, not a refusal"*. On a swap between two tails the longer of the two
lessors' notice periods applies. Gear and APU swaps are not engine removals and need no notice
under this clause.

## 12.3(c) — Temporary installation

Summary: another engine may be installed temporarily while an Engine is away for maintenance; the
removed Engine remains the Engine and is reinstalled.

**As applied** (`coverUntilRestored`): for an engine that runs out before handback, a pool spare
goes on when it runs out; the engine goes into the first slot the lead time allows, comes back after
the turnaround and is reinstalled before handback. It stays the permanent engine, so 12.2's
replacement test does not apply to the spare — which is how a spare the lease would not accept as
a permanent replacement can still cover. Priced: the shop visit, two removals and installations,
two overnight changes, and the spare's time away from the pool, as the life it burns on the tail
at a build-for-interval visit's rates. Reinstalling the engine is a planned removal of the spare,
with its 90 days' notice.

Every current lessor carries 90 days (`engineRemovalNoticeDays`).

## 12.6 — Title to replacements

Summary: title to the replacement passes to the lessor.

**As applied**: already the model's treatment — a spare installed as a replacement leaves the
airline with all its life above the thresholds (priced at a build-for-interval visit's rates), and
the removed unit goes to the pool, where its life stays the airline's.

## The qualified maintenance event clause

Held per lessor as `qmeClauseRef` / `qmeClauseText` (generated per lease template; see
`data/generate.ts`): maintenance not evidenced as the clause requires does not reset the interval.
